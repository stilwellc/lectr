Presented is a scarce Home Run Baker T206 card with an El Principe De Gales back. This example serves as the Baseball Hall of Famer's highest graded with this rare back in the PSA population report. The card features a beautiful image of the Philadelphia Athletics third baseman, with solid corners as well. The card is overall well centered left to right, and about 35/65 top to bottom.
